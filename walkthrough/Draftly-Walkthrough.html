Step 1 · Authentication

Sign in with a real seeded account

Demo switching uses Supabase Auth email/password — not a client-side impersonation dropdown. Alice is the document owner for this walkthrough.

Step 2 · Workspace

My Documents workspace

Sidebar navigation for My Documents and Shared with Me, search, Import File, and New Document. Empty/list states are handled in the UI.

Step 3 · Editor + autosave

Create, rename title, edit rich text

Created Q2 Product Strategy, typed content with heading formatting, and confirmed the visible Saved status after debounced autosave. Content is stored as Tiptap JSON.

Step 4 · Sharing

Share by email with role selection

Owner-only Share dialog: choose Viewer or Editor, share with a registered user email, and review people with access. Non-owners cannot manage sharing.

Step 5 · Collaborators

Bob = Editor, Carol = Viewer

Shared with bob@draftly.demo as editor and carol@draftly.demo as viewer. Access can be revoked immediately from this dialog.

Step 6 · Shared with Me

Recipient workspace shows the shared document

Signed in as Carol. The document appears under Shared with Me with owner name and viewer permission label.

Step 7 · Permission enforcement

Viewer is read-only (UI + server)

As Carol, title is disabled, formatting toolbar is disabled, Share is hidden, and content is view-only. Unauthorized writes are rejected server-side as well.
